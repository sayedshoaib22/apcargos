Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas in Hyderabad does AP Cargos serve?

AP Cargos serves all major areas in Hyderabad including Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Hitech City, LB Nagar, Uppal, Begumpet, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Secunderabad, Shamshabad, Dilsukhnagar, and Kompally. Contact us for specific area coverage details.

How can I book a cargo service?

You can book through our website, mobile app, or by calling our customer service team. Provide your cargo details, pickup location, and destination. We'll provide an instant quote and schedule your pickup at your convenience.

Is my cargo insured?

Yes, all shipments transported by AP Cargos are fully insured. We provide comprehensive coverage against loss or damage during pickup, transit, and delivery. Insurance terms are clearly outlined in our service agreement.

What is the delivery time for parcels in Hyderabad?

Local deliveries within Hyderabad typically take 24-48 hours depending on location and cargo type. Express options are available for urgent shipments. Delivery timeframe will be confirmed at booking.

Do you offer real-time tracking?

Yes, all shipments come with real-time GPS tracking. You can monitor your cargo's location and status through our website or mobile app anytime. SMS and email updates are also provided.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfer, online payment, credit/debit cards, and cheques. Flexible payment options are available for corporate and bulk shipments. EMI facilities may be available for select services.
