Our team unloads and places goods at your new location. You receive a digital delivery receipt with photographic proof. For office and home relocations, we also offer reassembly and placement as an optional service.

FAQs

Hyderabad Cargo Service — Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deliver cargo within Hyderabad? ▼
For intra-city cargo, our average delivery time is 3–5 hours from pickup. For time-sensitive deliveries within the same zone (e.g., Gachibowli to Madhapur, or LB Nagar to Uppal), we can complete delivery in as little as 2 hours.
Do you handle IT equipment and server relocations in Hyderabad? ▼
Yes — this is one of our specialised services in Hyderabad. We have staff trained in IT equipment handling, anti-static packing procedures, cable labelling and server room logistics. We've completed moves for companies in Madhapur, Hitech City and the Financial District.
Which areas in Hyderabad do you serve? ▼
We cover all major areas: Madhapur, Gachibowli, Kukatpally, Miyapur, LB Nagar, Secunderabad, Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Begumpet, Hitech City, Uppal, Dilsukhnagar, Kompally and Shamshabad — plus surrounding localities. If your area isn't listed, call us and we'll confirm coverage.
Can you ship from Hyderabad to Mumbai or Pune? ▼
Absolutely. We operate regular inter-state cargo runs on the Hyderabad–Mumbai and Hyderabad–Pune corridors, with trucks departing 3 times a week. You can book a full-load vehicle or share space in a part-load shipment — both are available at competitive rates.
How do I get a quick price for Hyderabad cargo? ▼
Call us directly or send a WhatsApp message with your pickup area, delivery area, type of goods and approximate quantity. We'll give you a firm quote within 5–10 minutes — no obligation. For businesses requiring regular shipments, ask about our contract pricing.
Is cargo insurance available for Hyderabad shipments? ▼
Yes. All AP Cargos shipments include basic transit insurance. For high-value goods — electronics, antiques, jewellery or expensive furniture — we recommend declaring the full value at booking so the insurance covers the actual replacement cost. There is no additional premium for declared values up to ₹1 lakh.
