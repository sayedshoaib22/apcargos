Frequently Asked Questions

How long does cargo take from Mumbai to Andhra Pradesh?

Standard door-to-door cargo takes 2–4 days depending on destination and load type. Guntur and Tirupati are usually 2–3 days; Rajahmundry usually 3–4 days.

Do you provide door-to-door pickup and delivery?

Yes — we collect from your home, shop or office in Mumbai and deliver directly to the recipient's address across Andhra Pradesh.

What items can I ship with AP Cargos?

We transport commercial cargo, parcels, household items and office equipment. We do not transport hazardous materials or items prohibited by law.

How is the cargo price calculated?

Pricing depends on weight, volume and destination. We provide a clear written quote before pickup; call or WhatsApp for an instant estimate.

Is my shipment insured during transit?

We take care in packing and handling; transit protection options are available on request. Discuss coverage with our team before booking.
