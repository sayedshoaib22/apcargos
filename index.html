Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does cargo take from Mumbai to Andhra Pradesh?

Standard door-to-door cargo from Mumbai to Guntur, Rajahmundry or Tirupati takes 2 to 4 days depending on the destination and load type. Call our team for an exact delivery date before booking.

Do you provide door-to-door pickup and delivery?

Yes. AP Cargos picks up your goods directly from your home, shop or office in Mumbai and delivers straight to the recipient's address in Guntur, Rajahmundry, Tirupati or anywhere in Andhra Pradesh. No depot visit is required.

What items can I send with AP Cargos?

We transport commercial cargo, parcels and packages, household goods during home shifting, office equipment during office relocation, and general goods transport for businesses. Share your item details on call or WhatsApp for a quote.

How is the cargo price calculated?

Pricing depends on weight, volume and destination. We share a clear, written quote before booking, with no hidden charges. Call our team for an instant estimate.

Is my shipment insured during transit?

We take full care in packing, loading and transporting your goods and offer transit protection options on request. Ask our team about coverage suited to your shipment before you book.
