Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions – Maharashtra Cargo Service

How long does cargo delivery take within Maharashtra? ▼
Within Mumbai and Thane, we offer same-day or next-day delivery. For cities like Pune and Nashik, transit takes 1–2 days. For longer routes to Nagpur, Amravati or Chandrapur, expect 2–3 business days depending on volume and route.
Do you offer door-to-door cargo service across Maharashtra? ▼
Yes — our primary offering is complete door-to-door service. We collect the goods from your home, office or warehouse and deliver directly to the recipient's address anywhere in Maharashtra without you needing to drop off at a hub.
What items are accepted for transport? ▼
We transport household goods, furniture, electronics, clothing, office equipment, commercial goods, industrial machinery, FMCG products, parcels and documents. We do not carry hazardous chemicals, flammable materials, or prohibited goods as per Indian transport law.
Is same-day pickup available in all Maharashtra cities? ▼
Same-day pickup is currently available in Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Kalyan for bookings made before 12 PM. For other Maharashtra cities, we offer next-day pickup with morning time slots. Contact us to confirm availability at your location.
How do you pack fragile or delicate items? ▼
Fragile items receive multi-layer protection: bubble wrap (at least 2 layers), foam padding, corrugated separators and outer cardboard boxing. For very delicate items like glass furniture, antiques or large electronics, we use custom wooden crating. Nothing leaves our hands until we're satisfied it's safe.
Can businesses use your cargo service for regular shipments? ▼
Absolutely. We offer monthly contracts and volume-based pricing for businesses with regular cargo needs. You get a dedicated account manager, priority scheduling, GST invoices and detailed monthly reports. Call us to discuss a custom corporate package.
