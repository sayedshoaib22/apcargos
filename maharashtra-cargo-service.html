Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does AP Cargos serve in Maharashtra?

We serve all major cities in Maharashtra including Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Nagpur, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Aurangabad, Kolhapur, Solapur, Jalgaon, Sangli, Satara, Ratnagiri, and Amravati. Contact us for specific city services.

How can I get a quote for my cargo?

You can get a quote by calling our customer service team, visiting our website, or filling out our online quote form. Provide details about your cargo, pickup location, and destination. We'll provide a competitive rate within hours.

Is my cargo insured during transport?

Yes, all cargo transported by AP Cargos is fully insured. Our insurance coverage protects against loss or damage during pickup, transit, and delivery. Insurance terms and limits are clearly outlined in our service agreement.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods including cash, bank transfer, online payment, credit/debit cards, and cheques. Flexible payment options are available for corporate clients and bulk shipments.

Can I track my shipment in real-time?

Yes, all shipments come with real-time GPS tracking. You can monitor your cargo's location and status through our website or mobile app. Updates are provided via SMS and email as well.
